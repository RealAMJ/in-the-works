Sweden men's national team

Mikko Riipinen's squad for the November 2026 World Cup qualifiers and where every player is playing right now.

Squad news: November window

Announced 3 November 2026 by the Swedish Basketball Federation.

Fourteen players are called up for the second round of World Cup qualifying: Hungary at home in Avicii Arena on Friday 27 November (19:00) and France away in Dijon on Monday 30 November (20:30). Twelve of the fourteen will play in each game.

Out: Pelle Larsson, the top scorer in the European qualifiers so far with 26.0 points a game, is with the Miami Heat, and the NBA does not stop for international windows. Ludvig Håkanson (19.7 points and 6.2 assists a game in the qualifiers) has withdrawn injured. Julius Price and Pierre Hampton from the August squad are not in this group.

In: Tobias Borg is back after missing August injured, Wilhelm Falk returns after playing the November 2025 and February 2026 windows, and 18-year-old Michael Odinks of Norrköping Dolphins gets his first senior call-up after a strong debut season in the SBL. Gustav Hansson of Jämtland Basket is also in.

The squad meets in Stockholm on 22 November and trains there until the Hungary game, then travels to France. More than 8,500 tickets have been sold for Sweden–Hungary.

Source: basket.se ↗
